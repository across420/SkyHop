# Sky Hop — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Sky Hop does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

**What stays on your device.** Your best score, number of games played, and your sound on/off setting are saved only on your iPhone so the game can remember them. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and deleting the app removes them.

**Game Center.** If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple to show on leaderboards. That data is handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not receive your Game Center information.

**No tracking, ads, or analytics.** Sky Hop contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party code.

**Children.** Sky Hop is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
